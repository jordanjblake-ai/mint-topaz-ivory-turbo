**Version 1.0 --- 20 August 2026**

**Working strategic document**

**Purpose:** Build a comprehensive understanding of Hybrid Vacations,
its people, products, customers, brand, marketing activity and growth
opportunities. This document is intended to become the foundational
knowledge base for the website redesign, marketing strategy and future
AI automation systems.

**1. Executive Summary**

Hybrid Vacations is a sports and travel business built around a simple
but potentially powerful combination:

**SPORT × TRAVEL × COMMUNITY × ADVENTURE**

The business currently has its strongest presence in **beach
volleyball**, with **tennis and padel** emerging as additional sports
verticals.

Hybrid\'s proposition goes beyond simply organising sports camps. The
brand combines:

-   Sporting development

-   Coaching

-   Travel

-   Destination experiences

-   Social connection

-   Community

-   Adventure

-   International sporting networks

There is also a significant **UK-based coaching and events
opportunity**, particularly around London. Mark Garcia-Kidd offers
private coaching sessions, clinics and mini-camps, with activity
primarily around London but potentially elsewhere in the UK.

This creates a much broader ecosystem than a conventional sports holiday
company.

**Working strategic interpretation**

Hybrid should ultimately be positioned as:

**A premium sports-travel and community brand that enables people to
travel through the sports they love.**

The strongest opportunity is to make **HYBRID** the master brand, with
beach volleyball, tennis, padel, coaching and other future sports
operating as connected parts of the same ecosystem.

**2. What Is Hybrid Vacations?**

**Current perception**

Hybrid Vacations currently presents itself primarily as a company
providing sports-focused holidays and experiences.

Its social presence is particularly strong around:

-   Beach volleyball

-   Lanzarote

-   Coaching

-   International destinations

-   Community

-   Athlete/coach personalities

-   Sports camps

However, the business appears to be evolving rapidly.

The addition of tennis and padel, alongside UK coaching, clinics,
mini-camps and performance activity, suggests that Hybrid is becoming a
**broader sports lifestyle business**.
