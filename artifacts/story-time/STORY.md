# Story Time

Sixteen months from a name to a world-tour court. The Hybrid story, still unfinished.

Live at `/story-time` on hybridvacations.com. Scroll. Optional sound. Tap to unlock.

## How two beats are meant to read

**The book, June 2025.** Someone writes HYBRYD on the cover, realises, rubs it out, then draws the official Hybrid Vacations wordmark. The misspelling is the draft. It is not a site typo.

**The last frame, 2027.** Bottom right says Designed by, then a drawn Jordan Blake signature (`art/credit-jordan-blake.png`). The name is the handwriting, not missing type.

**The empty court.** “The court is still empty on purpose.” The story is open. That line stays.
